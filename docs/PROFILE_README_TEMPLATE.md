# Maxim Zavrazhnov

PhD student at **Peter the Great St. Petersburg Polytechnic University (SPbPU)** with a background in applied mathematics, systems analysis and data-driven project management.

I work on reproducible research pipelines, predictive modeling and cooperative game-theoretic methods for project-team analysis. I am also expanding my practical stack toward **cybersecurity, AI agents, Linux/networking and engineering automation**.

## Selected projects

### [Team Formation Pipeline](https://github.com/maximzavrazhnov/team-formation-pipeline)
Python research prototype for data-driven project-team formation, coalition search and Shapley-inspired allocation.

### [Gryzzly Team Composition Reproducibility](https://github.com/maximzavrazhnov/gryzzly-team-composition-reproducibility)
Point-in-time project-team composition research with temporal validation, partial pooling, fixed-cardinality optimization and Shapley attribution.

### [TAWOS Temporal Integrity Analysis](https://github.com/maximzavrazhnov/tawos-temporal-integrity-analysis)
Reproducible analysis of temporal integrity, leakage risk and cross-project model transferability in software-project data.

### [Weighted Network Configurator — C++ / Qt](https://github.com/maximzavrazhnov/weighted-network-configurator-qt)
C++/Qt desktop application for enumerating, ranking and visualizing feasible weighted network configurations under degree constraints.
